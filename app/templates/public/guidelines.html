Community Guidelines & Responsible Use

CivicConnect is built to foster constructive community dialogue and data-backed local improvements. To ensure fairness and privacy, all users must adhere to these standards.

1. Authentic, Constructive Reporting

  • No False or Fabricated Reports: Only report conditions that you have directly observed. Deliberately submitting misleading or fictitious problems disrupts volunteer efforts and degrades data integrity.
  • Check for Existing Reports: Before creating a new submission, glance at the Public Issue directory to avoid duplicating existing entries. Duplicate reports will be flagged and consolidated.
  • Accurate Details: Provide objective, factual descriptions (e.g. "large pothole on outer perimeter road") rather than vague complaints.

2. Privacy Protection & Personal Information

  • No Personal Contact Information: Do NOT enter personal phone numbers, private email addresses, or full personal names in the report description or area fields.
  • No Exact Home Addresses: Provide neighborhood references, road junctions, or campus landmarks only. Never publish private residential flat numbers.
  • Photo Metadata Stripping: All photos uploaded to the platform have their EXIF / GPS metadata permanently stripped upon receipt to prevent location tracking.

3. Zero Tolerance for Abusive or Defamatory Content

  • No Targeted Harassment: Reports accusing specific individuals, employees, or neighbors by name are strictly prohibited. Such submissions will be hidden immediately.
  • Civil Language: Profanity, hate speech, threats, and derogatory remarks will result in immediate removal and rate-limiting.

4. Understanding Resolution & Limitations

This platform is a student-led initiative for a Community Engagement Project (CEP). Marking an issue as Resolved indicates that student volunteers or project members have verified local rectification (e.g., litter cleanups, placement of caution signs, or verification of repaired fixtures). It does not signify official municipality or government legal sign-off.
